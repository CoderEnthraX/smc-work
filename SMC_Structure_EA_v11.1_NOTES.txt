4. HOW IT TRADES (timing - the same as TradingView)
---------------------------------------------------
- Everything is decided at the CLOSE of each candle (in MT5: on the
  first price of the next candle), like TradingView's bar-close
  strategy.
- An order decided at a candle close works from the next candle.
  Example (1 minute): the 10:05 candle closes with a CHOCH up. At
  10:06:00 the EA arms the buy at the RULE 1 level, e.g. 2345.20.
- Stops and targets are real orders on every trade at the broker, so
  a trade is protected even if the EA or the VPS stops.
- Closes (02:00 force close, weekend, news window, opposite signal)
  go at the first price of the next candle - where TradingView fills
  them too.
- Moved stops (group 32 step / break-even, trailing stop) are changed
  at the candle close.

6. ENTRY MODES (group 40 "How entries are executed")
----------------------------------------------------
TOUCH (default) - "Like TradingView": the EA watches the price. When
  the chart price (bid) reaches the entry level, it sends a market
  order with the stop and target. This is TradingView's fill rule and
  what TheConnector does today. A buy is filled at the ask, so you pay
  the spread on the entry. Needs the EA running (VPS).
PENDING - a buy limit / sell limit waits at the broker. A buy limit
  fills only when the ASK reaches it (with a spread of 0.20 the price
  must go 0.20 lower than on TradingView). Orders stay at the broker
  when the EA is off - but then nobody closes trades at 02:00 or on
  Friday.
In both modes: if the price is already through the entry when the
order is sent (a gap), the EA buys / sells at once at market, as
TradingView fills at the open.
Every price is rounded to the broker's 0.01 step the safe way: a buy
entry and a buy stop down, a buy target up (the opposite for a sell),
so an order fills only when TradingView's would. Tested: this changes
no trade.


7. HEDGE MODE (group 33)
------------------------
The EA runs two sides on the same market structure:
  LONG side  - takes only buy signals (like a TradingView copy set to
               "Longs only")
  SHORT side - takes only sell signals ("Shorts only")
Each side follows every rule by itself: its own waiting setup, its
own trades, close-and-reverse closes only its own trades.

Example (close and reverse OFF):
  10:05 CHOCH up    -> LONG side buys at 2345.20, stop 2338.70
  10:40 CHOCH down  -> the long trade stays open; SHORT side sells at
                       2352.80, stop 2359.30. Both trades are open.
  Net exposure can be zero - that is normal in a hedge.

Loss recovery, account floor, pause after losses, daily limits:
  "Each side its own"  - each side has its own memory (exactly two
                         TradingView copies). Checked: identical to
                         a Longs-only + a Shorts-only run.
  "Shared by both"     - one memory for the account. Example with
                         Rule C, split 1, base 50: the long side loses
                         120 -> the next trade on EITHER side risks
                         120 (Rule C risks the loss carried).
                         The pause counts losses from both sides in the
                         order they closed. Max trades per day counts
                         both sides.
What the tests said (2021-2026, 1 minute, your entries): hedge doubled
the losses (-30,771 vs -18,514); shared Rule C emptied a 10,000
account in every version. I do not recommend hedge with loss recovery.


8. DIFFERENCES FROM TRADINGVIEW (honest list)
---------------------------------------------
1. Prices: FxPro is not OANDA (about +0.25 USD). Different candles can
   give different swings. Earlier test: 85 of ~114 trades identical in
   3 months; all differences came from the price feed.
2. Costs: real spread, commission and swap. TradingView has no spread.
   A sell's stop and target are triggered by the ASK (a little early
   for the stop, a little late for the target).
3. Loss recovery adds up the REAL money of each trade (profit +
   commission + swap). TradingView uses its own commission estimate.
4. "loss recovery counts from": "the whole history" = every trade of
   this EA (same magic, same symbol) on this account; "when the EA
   first started" = saved on the first start (in the Strategy Tester:
   the test start); "a date I choose" = UTC.
5. Group 23 (commission) is used only to SIZE trades and to push the
   target out, as in TradingView. Copy your TradingView values.
6. The structure is built from the last 20,000 bars, not the whole
   chart. After the first few swings both agree.
7. When you attach the EA it waits for the next signal. A setup that
   was armed earlier in history is not taken.
8. Higher timeframe = MT5's own 15m / 1h / 4h / 1D candles of the last
   CLOSED candle. FxPro's 4h candles start at 17:00 New York, like
   TradingView's OANDA candles.
9. Minimum / maximum lot: the broker's. A size below 0.01 lot is
   skipped (counted as "skipped (stop size)"); a size above the
   maximum is cut to the maximum.
10. TradingView-only items are not in the EA: the webhook, the "refused
   by TradingView" and "missed stop" safety nets and the audit labels
   (the EA writes them in the Experts tab when "Signal audit" is ON).
   The counter table is there, with MT5 rows (section 14).

10. RESTARTS (VPS reboot, terminal update, settings change)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Every candle the EA saves its waiting setup in MT5 (Tools > Global
  Variables, names start with SMC<magic>_<symbol>_).
- After a restart of up to 5 missed candles it carries on exactly as if
  nothing happened (tested: 484 restarts, identical results).
- More than 5 missed candles: the waiting setup is dropped and old
  pending orders of this EA are deleted; it waits for the next signal.
  Open trades keep their stops and targets and are still managed
  (02:00 close, weekend, stop moves).
- The loss memory (Rule A / B / C), floor, pause and daily counts are
  rebuilt from the account's trade history at every start.
- Removing the EA from the chart deletes its pending orders.

  17:50 - 18:20 IST  no trading, an open trade is closed at 17:50

NO NEW TRADES BEFORE NEWS (your idea)
From X hours before a release until the release: no NEW trade.
  - a waiting order is cancelled
    ("CANCELLED - blocked (news soon - no new trades)")
  - a new CHOCH / BOS is skipped ("SKIP - news soon - no new trades")
  - a trade that is ALREADY open keeps its stop and target and runs
    on; the news window itself (above) then closes it, if set so
Example: CPI at 18:00 IST, hours 1, window 10 / 20:
  17:00 - 17:50  no new trade, open trades run
  17:50 - 18:20  news window: open trades closed, no trading
  18:20          normal again
