4. SECTION 1 - ONE UNIT FOR EVERY PRICE DISTANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Unit for every price distance            Price  (Price / Pips / % of price)
     - pip size                               0      (0 = automatic)

The unit is read by FOUR boxes:
   section 5   Stop buffer beyond the CHOCH* level
   section 4   Skip the setup if the stop is CLOSER than
   section 4   Skip the setup if the stop is FURTHER than
   section 3   RULE 3 - the most the close may be beyond the broken level
   section 11  Broker spread for SIZING
Money boxes (risk, caps, floor, daily loss) stay in account currency - they
mean the same on every market.

PRICE (default) - exactly as before: 1.0 = 1.00 of price.
PIPS - the strategy knows the pip of the market: gold 0.10, silver 0.01,
  Bitcoin 1, Ethereum 0.10, forex 0.0001, JPY pairs 0.01, anything else
  10 x the smallest price step (or type your own pip size).
% OF PRICE - worked out again for every setup:
  the stop buffer = % of the CHOCH* level,
  closer / further = % of the entry price,
  rule 3 distance  = % of the signal candle's close,
  spread           = % of the price.

Example - "% of price", buffer 0.025, closer 0.5, further 1.0:
   market            buffer      skip if the stop is closer / further
   gold at 4,000     1.00        20.00 / 40.00
   gold at 2,000     0.50        10.00 / 20.00
   silver at 50      0.0125      0.25 / 0.50
   EURUSD at 1.10    0.000275    0.0055 / 0.011
   Bitcoin 100,000   25          500 / 1,000
One set of numbers works on every market and at every price level.

ONE STOP BUFFER BOX. Up to v12.3 there were three boxes: "stop buffer in
price" (group 20), "in pips" and "in %" (group 38). Now there is one box
in section 5, read in the section 1 unit. When you change the unit, TYPE
THE NUMBERS AGAIN - 1.0 in price is not 1.0 pips and not 1.0 %:
   your v12 setting                       v13.0
   Price, buffer 1.0                      Price, buffer 1.0  (nothing to do)
   Pips, "stop buffer in pips" 10         Pips, buffer 10
   % of price, "stop buffer in %" 0.02    % of price, buffer 0.02
The table row "UNIT + STOP BUFFER (sections 1, 5)" shows every distance in
price, for example "0.025% of the level = 1.00 now | skip closer 0.5 %".
TradingView stops with a message, and the EA refuses to start, if the
unit is % and the stop buffer or the spread is above 10 (a typing slip).

THE SPREAD FOR SIZING (section 11) now follows the unit too. Up to v12.3 it
was always price. With the unit on Price nothing changes.

5. EA - HEDGE + SHARED: "A TRADE OPENED WHILE THE OTHER SIDE CHASES THE
   LOSS RISKS ONLY THE BASE" (section 3, OFF)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Trade direction                            Hedge
     - hedge: loss recovery, floor ...         Shared by both sides
     - hedge + shared: ... risks only the base OFF

Only with Hedge + Shared money + a loss-recovery rule (A / B / C, the +
and split versions). OFF = exactly as v12.3.

The problem: with one shared memory, a buy and a sell open at the same
time are BOTH sized to win back the same carried loss.
The rule: while one side "chases" the loss (its waiting order or open
trade was sized for it), a new trade on the OTHER side risks only the
base. The side that was set up first keeps the chase; on the same candle,
the buy side. When the chasing trade closes, the next trade gets the full
Rule A / B / C amount again.

Example - Rule C split 1, base 50, target 1:1, 100 carried.
A buy opens risking 100, then a sell signal comes while it is open.
   buy    sell   v12.3: result / carried    v13.0 ON: result / carried
   win    win    +200 / 0                   +150 / 0
   win    loss      0 / 100                  +50 / 50
   loss   win       0 / 100                  -50 / 150
   loss   loss   -200 / 300                 -150 / 250
At risk at once: 200 before, 150 now. The average is the same - it only
makes the swings smaller. It does not make the entries profitable.

Table: the "Trade direction (section 3)" row shows how many trades were
sized at the base ("2nd trade at the base: N"). The journal / audit label
says "L7 at the BASE risk - the other side is chasing the loss".
Restarts keep it: the flags are saved with the waiting setup in MT5's
global variables (an open trade's flag always).
"Send once" entry handling keeps the size the order was sent with.

6. EA - FASTER TABLE AND DRAWINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The trading logic was already fast. On a live chart (and in the VISUAL
Strategy Tester) most of the EA's time went into the table and the
drawings, redrawn at every candle. Now:
  - every table cell remembers what it shows - the EA sends a text, colour
    or position to the chart only when it CHANGED;
  - lines and texts set their fixed look (style, width, size, anchor)
    once, when they are made, not at every candle.
The NON-visual Strategy Tester never drew anything (unchanged).
Measured in my MT5 test copy, one year of 1-minute bars, table + drawings
ON:    v12.3   32.9 s of computer time   (hedge: 35.5 s)
   v13.0   22.0 s                    (hedge: 24.8 s)  - about 1/3 less
   (the trading part alone: 4.1 s - the rest is the table and drawings)
On a live chart the table is drawn once a minute, so you will not feel
it there; it matters most in the VISUAL Strategy Tester.
Nothing else changed: the trades with the table / drawings ON are still
the same as with them OFF.

7. EA - BUGS FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
All four are settings that were not checked; the EA now refuses to start
with a clear message (MT5 shows it in a pop-up and in the Experts tab):
  a. A news window time that could not be read (for example 17:25-18:35
     instead of 1725-1835, or 2500-2600) was IGNORED without a word - the
     window never blocked anything. Now: "Window 1 time (section 10) must
     look like 1725-1835".
  b. A NEGATIVE max slippage became an unlimited slippage (MT5 reads it
     as an unsigned number). Now 0 - 100000 points.
  c. A negative magic number. Now 0 or more.
  d. Negative "keep a waiting setup after a restart" bars or marks to
     keep. Now 0 or more.
TradingView: the time picker of the windows never allowed a bad time, so
nothing to fix there.
